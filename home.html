Calendar

Shows a Google Calendar you both share, so you can add events from the Google Calendar app on your phones as well as here. It connects through a small script that runs in your Google account:

  1. On a computer, open calendar-bridge.gs and follow the steps at the top. You'll paste it into script.google.com, set a secret phrase, and deploy it as a web app.
  2. Paste the Web app URL and the same secret phrase here, then tap Connect.

Holidays and birthdays need the latest calendar-bridge.gs (see step 7 in it for birthdays).

Alexa

Goodnight and the Alexa buttons go through Voice Monkey, a free Alexa skill:

  1. Sign up at voicemonkey.io and enable the Voice Monkey skill in the Alexa app.
  2. In the Voice Monkey console, add a Routine Trigger for each button (or a Speaker for announcements) and copy its ID.
  3. In the Alexa app, make a Routine. For When this happens, pick the Voice Monkey device and that trigger, then add the actions.

A button can also switch the curtain and strip to a scene after Alexa runs, or only do the lights if you leave the ID empty.

Goodnight

Runs an Alexa routine straight away, then turns the curtain and strip off. Copy the actions from your "Alexa, goodnight" routine into a routine that starts from a Voice Monkey trigger.

Lights

USB lights (Zengge BT SPI controller)

Same as "Number of IC light per segment" in the ZENGGE app.

Full-length test

Each button sends a rainbow along all the LEDs, split up a different way. Tap 1. If one rainbow runs from start to end with nothing dark, you're done. If not, try 2, then 3, and so on. The one you tap last is kept.

When pairing, the list may show the curtain too (they're the same make). The controller is the one that isn't the curtain's name; if you pick the curtain by mistake it'll tell you.

Colour check

If colours on these lights come out wrong (red shows as green, for example), tap Check colours and answer the two questions. Every colour sent to them is then corrected.

Each light takes one Bluetooth connection at a time, so while this screen is connected your phone can't be.

To reconnect by itself after the page reloads, open chrome://flags, search for Web Bluetooth new permissions backend, turn it on and restart Chrome.
