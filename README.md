# Curtain Lights

A phone remote for Electight / Surplife Bluetooth LED curtain lights (3 × 3 m, 300 LEDs).

Open the page in **Chrome on Android** (or Chrome/Edge on a computer), tap **Connect** and pick the curtain.
It talks to the lights directly over Web Bluetooth, so no app or PC is needed.

- Solid colours with brightness, white and warm white
- Built-in effects with speed and brightness
- A painter for setting individual LEDs, with a Locate tool for matching the LED layout

The Bluetooth protocol comes from the community reverse-engineering notes at
https://zff.dev/ge0rg/surplife-curtain. Effects and per-LED painting are best guesses until tested.
